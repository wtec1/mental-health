Our Direction

Our Goals

Clear, measurable objectives guiding our mission to improve youth mental health across Malawi.

Education

Provide Mental Health Education in Schools

Deliver structured, youth-friendly mental health education programs that equip students with knowledge, awareness, and coping skills.

Access

Promote Accessible Mental Health Information

Ensure accurate, stigma-free mental health information is available both physically and digitally across communities.

Awareness

Conduct Awareness Workshops

Facilitate interactive workshops in schools, workplaces, and communities to address stigma and encourage help-seeking behavior.

Collaboration

Collaborate with Professionals

Partner with medical experts, psychologists, institutions, and organizations to strengthen mental health delivery systems.

Prevention

Support Suicide Prevention Initiatives

Advocate for early intervention, crisis response training, and community-based prevention strategies targeting youth.
