Who We Are

A Registered Mental Health
Initiative for Malawi's Youth

LEHEMA — Let's Talk Mental Health Malawi — was established in 2020 during the height of the COVID-19 pandemic. Recognizing the significant gaps in mental health support and awareness across Malawi, our founder set out to build something different: an initiative rooted in love, accessible to all, and relentlessly focused on youth.

We work to bridge disparities in mental health care delivery by providing mental health first aid, addressing stigma, raising awareness, and ensuring that affected individuals and communities receive the assistance they need to lead healthier, fulfilling lives.

Why We Exist

Rising mental health challenges among Malawian youth

Deep-rooted stigma and widespread misinformation

Limited access to affordable, quality psychosocial support
